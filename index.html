Frequently asked questions

What is Fret Not?

Fret Not is a free iOS guitar songbook app for beginners. It tracks every song you've learned — chords, capo positions, strumming patterns, and tuning — so you never forget how to play them.

Is Fret Not free?

Yes. Fret Not is completely free on iPhone and iPad, with no ads and no in-app purchases. It's a side project — I'm learning to play guitar and learning to build apps, and I'm continuously making Fret Not better. Feedback and ideas are always welcome.

What platforms does Fret Not support?

Fret Not is available for iPhone and iPad. It requires iOS 17 or later. There is no Android or web version.

Is Fret Not good for beginners?

Yes. Fret Not was built specifically for beginner guitarists who want a simple, structured way to track the songs they're learning — without the ads and clutter of larger guitar apps.

Can I import songs from Spotify into Fret Not?

Yes. You can import songs directly from your Spotify library into Fret Not, then add the chords, capo, strumming pattern, and other details as you learn them. You can also play the song on Spotify directly from inside the app, so you can play along while practicing.

Can I import songs from Ultimate Guitar?

Yes. Share any song from the Ultimate Guitar app and Fret Not imports the chords, capo position, and lyrics directly into your songbook.

Does Fret Not tell me which chords are in a song?

Not yet — automatic chord detection is a feature we're working on. For now, Fret Not suggests likely chords based on the song's key and capo position, but you'll need to know the chords yourself when adding a song manually. Alternatively, import the song from Ultimate Guitar to bring in the chords and lyrics automatically.

Does Fret Not have a chord identifier and tuner?

Yes. Fret Not includes a virtual fretboard chord identifier (tap notes to identify any chord) and a built-in high-precision guitar tuner.

Who made Fret Not?

Fret Not was built by Alexa Kaminsky, a product designer learning to play guitar. The first version was built in one week using Claude Code, and the app launched in December 2025.
