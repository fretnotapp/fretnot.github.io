How it works

Set up a song once, then just play

Save each song with its chords, capo, strumming and tuning, keep your whole songbook in one place, and add new ones in seconds.

Add a song, then add the chords

It takes two steps. First add the song: search for it, type it in, or share it in from wherever you found it. Then add the chords: type them in by hand for free, or let Fret Not Pro suggest them for you. It picks chords for your skill level, with the capo that keeps even a hard song in easy, open shapes.

The rest of what you get

A reference for every chord

Browse the full chord library any time, not just the chords in your songs. Every one comes with a picture of where your fingers go.

Chord identifier

Holding a shape you cannot name? Tap it onto the fretboard and Fret Not tells you what it is, then saves it to a song or your own library.

Built-in tuner

Real-time pitch detection, string by string, with alternate tunings supported. No second app to go and find.

iCloud sync, no account

Your songs follow you from iPhone to iPad through your own iCloud, so your songbook is the same on every device.

Build a practice streak

Fret Not counts every day you play and keeps your streak alive, with a nudge the evening before you would break it. The habit is what carries you past those first months.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Fret Not?
Fret Not is a free iOS app for the songs you’re learning on guitar. Save each song with its chords, capo, strumming and tuning in one place, so weeks later you can pull it up and play instead of learning it from scratch again.

Does Fret Not teach you to play?
No, and that is on purpose. Fret Not is for people who already know a few chords and want to keep playing. For technique, a teacher or a video course will serve you better. This is what you open on the days in between.

What is Fret Not Pro?
Pro looks up the chords for any song you add and matches them to your skill level. Instead of the barre chords other apps hand you, it suggests easy open shapes and the capo to play them with, so a song like Jolene comes back beginner-friendly at capo 4 rather than as C#m and B. It is $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and everything else in the app stays free.

How do I add chords to a song?
Two ways. For free, type them in yourself, and every chord you enter gets a full shape diagram. Or with Fret Not Pro, they are looked up for you automatically, matched to your skill level and with the capo to play them.

Will it show me where to put my fingers?
Yes. Every chord comes with a diagram showing exactly where your fingers go, numbered finger by finger, so you are never left guessing a shape.

Will Pro know my song?
Almost always, including plenty of less common and non-English songs. A small number of very obscure songs cannot be found automatically, and you can always add those chords by hand.

What if the chords Pro suggests aren’t right?
You can fix any chord by hand, and your change is saved to the song. Pro checks its suggestions against the song’s detected key and improves over time, but you always decide what your song ends up with.

Is there a free trial?
There is no separate trial. The app itself is free: add as many songs as you like, use the tuner and chord library, and type in chords yourself. Pro adds the automatic chord and capo suggestions on top.

Can I cancel Pro?
Any time, from your Apple subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

If I cancel Pro, do I lose the chords it found?
No. Any chords Pro looked up are saved onto your songs and keep showing after you cancel. You just cannot run new lookups until you resubscribe.

How do you record strumming patterns for each song?
Each song has a strumming field where you write the pattern down in whatever shorthand you already use, such as D D U U D U. It sits next to the chords, so you get the rhythm and the shapes in one glance.

Does Fret Not support alternate tunings?
Yes. Each song can use standard tuning or an alternate like Drop D or Open G, and the built-in tuner is tuning-aware, so it tunes each string to whatever tuning the song is set to.

Can I add my own chord shapes?
Yes. Create custom chords with the built-in chord identifier, and pick a different voicing for any chord in a song.

Can I play the song while I practice?
Yes. Any song you link to Spotify plays straight from Fret Not, so you can hear the record and strum along with the chords on screen. Songs you add from Spotify come in with their album art, and you can import a whole playlist at once.

Do guitar chord charts work offline?
Yes. Your songs and their chord shapes live on your device, so they open with no connection at all. Looking up chords for a brand new song with Pro needs the internet, but everything already saved does not.

Do I need an account?
No. Pro is an in-app purchase through the App Store, so it follows your Apple ID. Your songs live on your device and in your own iCloud, and we never sell your personal data.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Yes, through your own iCloud, with no account or sign-in. Add a song on one device and it shows up on the other.

Does the tuner record me?
No. The tuner listens through your microphone to detect pitch in real time. The audio is processed on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
